📜 VELKARYA COMMUNITY RULES

🌍 General Rules

Velkarya is a free roleplay world where players can create countries, kingdoms, and factions. All actions must be consistent with established lore.

⚔️ War Rules

Wars must have valid reasons (casus belli), must go through process, and cannot be instant wins or godmod.

🤝 Diplomacy Rules

All treaties must be agreed by both sides. Betrayal is allowed but must be logical in RP.

🧠 Roleplay Conduct

Keep roleplay and outside discussions separate. Do not mix them.

⚖️ Conduct Rules

No hate speech, no personal attacks, and respect all players.

📌 Lore Rules

All lore must be consistent and explained within the universe.

🚫 NSFW Rules

No NSFW content allowed. Romance must stay light and non-explicit.

🔥 Final Rule

Velkarya is about creativity, storytelling, and worldbuilding together.
